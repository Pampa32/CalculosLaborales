## Umbrales de semaforo
- Verde: CTR CTA >= 2.0% y CVR lead >= 0.8% y Ratio lead/clic >= 20%
- Amarillo: CTR CTA 1.0%-1.9% o CVR lead 0.3%-0.79% o Ratio lead/clic 10%-19%
- Rojo: CTR CTA < 1.0% o CVR lead < 0.3% o Ratio lead/clic < 10%

Formulas:
- CTR CTA principal = cta_primary_click / sesiones
- CVR lead = lead_form_submit / sesiones
- Ratio lead/clic = lead_form_submit / cta_primary_click
